# ✨ Mod Introduction

This mod automatically collects books that players have read, and allows you to quickly view, edit, and categorize them directly from your inventory. Once a book is collected, you don't need to carry it with you—you can preview its contents right in the inventory interface.

## 📚 Book Management

- **Quick Share (Single Target):** Players can press the `G` key while aiming at another player to quickly share collected books with them.
- **Area Share (Group):** Press the "Share" button in the inventory interface to share within an area.
  - The sharing range covers all players within **5 blocks** centered on yourself.
  - Players who receive the share request can press the `Y` key to accept, or the `N` key to decline.
- **Categorization:** In the inventory interface, you can drag collected books to quickly group and organize them.
